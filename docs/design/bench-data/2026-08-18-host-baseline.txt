Summary
-------
Measured mean: 14.75 ms (range 12–17 ms, n=8).  This is the authoritative
host-native baseline.  The ~10 ms figure that appeared in earlier records
(bench.sh, TBD-PD-17, D-PD-100, D-PD-101) was a timer-resolution artifact:
/usr/bin/time -f %e and the shell `time` builtin report at 0.01 s granularity,
which cannot distinguish 10 ms from 17 ms.  Superseded by D-PD-103.
Correct multipliers (ratio of means): virtiofs ~10× host-native (149.51/14.75);
ext4 ~2× host-native (31.52/14.75).  In-guest ratio 4.74× (auto)/4.53×
(writeback) is unchanged.
